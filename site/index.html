How it works

From voice note to your book,
in seconds.

  1. Speak your trade

    Send a WhatsApp voice note. Say what you sold, what you spent, or who owes you.

  2. Veyra listens

    Veyra hears your voice and writes it down — in your language, even with market noise.

  3. It’s in the book

    Your sale is saved right away. Made a mistake? Just say “no, it was ₦4,000” and Veyra fixes it.

  4. Know your week

    Every week, Veyra sends your profit and who still owes you — right in the same chat.
